Privacy Policy

Sven task assistant  ·  Last updated: September 8, 2026

This policy covers the text messaging service operated by James Cederstav ("we", "us") for personal task and reminder management. The service is run by an individual, not a company, and is used by the operator and a small group of approved colleagues.

Information we collect

We collect only what the service needs to work:

  • Mobile phone numbers of the operator and approved participants
  • The content of text messages sent to and from the service, which consists of task descriptions, due dates, and confirmations
  • Basic delivery metadata provided by our messaging carrier, such as timestamps and delivery status

How we use it

Information is used solely to record tasks, send reminders and confirmations back to the person who sent them, and maintain the operator's personal schedule. It is not used for marketing, advertising, profiling, or resale of any kind.

Sharing of mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never sold, rented, or shared with any third party.

The only parties with access to message data are the operator and the messaging infrastructure providers required to deliver messages, who process it strictly on our behalf and may not use it for their own purposes.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies with the number of tasks in progress, and is typically fewer than ten messages per day per participant. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact the operator directly using the address below. Opting out removes your number from the approved list and ends all messages to you.

Data retention

Task records are kept only while the operator needs them for scheduling and are deleted when no longer useful. You may request deletion of your number and any associated messages at any time by contacting us.

Security

Data is stored in access-controlled systems available only to the operator. No participant data is published or made available to anyone outside the approved list.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a revised date.
