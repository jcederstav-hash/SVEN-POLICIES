About this service

Sven is a personal task and reminder service operated by James Cederstav, an individual. Participants text task requests to the service number and receive confirmations and reminders in return. The service is not a commercial product, is not offered to the public, and sends no marketing or promotional messages.

Who can participate

Participation is limited to the operator and a small number of colleagues who have verbally agreed to take part. Numbers are added manually to an approved list. Anyone not on that list can neither send messages to the service nor receive messages from it.

Consent

By verbally agreeing to participate and providing your mobile number, you consent to receive text messages related to task scheduling and reminders. Consent is not a condition of employment or of any purchase, and you may withdraw it at any time.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies with the number of tasks in progress, and is typically fewer than ten messages per day. Message and data rates may apply.

Any charges come from your own mobile carrier under your existing plan. The operator does not charge participants anything.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP to any message to be removed from the approved list and stop all further messages. You will receive one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and nothing after that. To rejoin later, ask the operator directly.
